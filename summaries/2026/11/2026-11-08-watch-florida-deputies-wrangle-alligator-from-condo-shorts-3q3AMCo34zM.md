## Summary
Bodycam footage shows Florida deputies from the Indian River County Sheriff's Office wrangling an alligator displaced by flooding from a condo parking garage in Sebastian, Florida, before successfully securing and transporting it.

Deputies in Sebastian, Florida, used ropes and specialized gear to capture an alligator that had been displaced by flooding and wandered into a condo parking area, eventually loading it into a trailer.
