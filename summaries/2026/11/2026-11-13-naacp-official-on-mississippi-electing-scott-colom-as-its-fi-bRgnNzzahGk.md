# Democrat Scott Colom Wins Mississippi Senate Seat

- **Published:** 2026-11-13
- **Channel:** CBS News
- **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bRgnNzzahGk)

## Summary
CBS News projects Democrat Scott Colom has won the Mississippi Senate race, defeating incumbent Republican Cindy Hyde-Smith and securing a historic victory as the first Black U.S. senator from Mississippi since the late 1800s. NAACP Mississippi State Conference Executive Director Charles Taylor discusses the historic voter mobilization efforts that contributed to the win.

## Major Developments
### 1. Scott Colom Wins Mississippi Senate Seat
Democratic district attorney Scott Colom defeated incumbent Republican Cindy Hyde-Smith, giving Democrats 55 Senate seats following a close race.

**Why it matters:** This marks a historic milestone as Mississippi elects its first Black U.S. senator since the late 1800s, reflecting significant voter engagement and a shift in representation.

### 2. NAACP Voter Mobilization Efforts in Mississippi
Charles Taylor, executive director of the Mississippi State Conference of the NAACP, detailed extensive outreach efforts, including knocking on 150,000 doors across 40 counties and engaging voters through radio, text messages, and community visits.

**Why it matters:** Intensive grassroots organizing and voter registration drives played a critical role in driving turnout and securing a close margin victory for the historic Senate win.

## Key Takeaways
- Democrat Scott Colom defeated incumbent Republican Cindy Hyde-Smith in Mississippi.
- Colom is the first Black U.S. senator elected from Mississippi since the late 1800s.
- The NAACP state conference heavily invested in voter engagement, visiting thousands of churches and opening multiple registration offices.
- Voters were largely motivated by economic concerns such as high gas and grocery prices, as well as the protection of democratic rights and voting access.
