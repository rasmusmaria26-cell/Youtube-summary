# Tracking Chinese Usage of American-Made A.I. Chips

- **Published:** 2026-11-21
- **Channel:** CBS News
- **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zD4gOHUOWEE)

## Summary
Washington has banned the export of Nvidia's most advanced AI chips to China, but Chinese tech firms may be remotely accessing these chips through data centers in Southeast Asia, such as Malaysia.

## Major Developments
### 1. Chinese Remote Access to Advanced U.S. AI Chips
Despite U.S. bans on exporting Nvidia's most advanced AI chips to China, Chinese tech companies are finding workarounds by remotely accessing computing power through data centers located in Southeast Asia, particularly Malaysia.

**Why it matters:** It reveals potential loopholes in current export controls designed to restrict China's access to cutting-edge AI computing power.

## Key Takeaways
- The U.S. has banned exports of Nvidia's top AI chips to China.
- Malaysia has emerged as a major data center hub experiencing massive growth.
- Geopolitical tensions are forcing Asian nations to navigate choices between the U.S. and China in AI trade and investment.
