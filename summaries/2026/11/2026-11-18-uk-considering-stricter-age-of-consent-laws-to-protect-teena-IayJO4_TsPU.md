# UK Considers Stricter Age of Consent Laws

- **Published:** 2026-11-18
- **Channel:** CBS News

## Summary
The UK is considering implementing a staggered age of consent to protect teenagers following allegations against high-profile figures involving relationships with young people.

## Major Developments
### 1. Staggered Age of Consent Discussion in the UK
Activists and the British government are discussing a staggered age of consent law to limit the age gap between partners when individuals are 16 or 17 years old.

**Why it matters:** It aims to protect teenagers from much older adults while addressing existing legal gaps where 16-year-olds can legally consent to adults of any age.

## Key Takeaways
- The current age of consent in the UK is 16.
- Similar laws, sometimes called 'Romeo and Juliet laws', exist elsewhere, such as Florida.
- Critics argue that focusing solely on age gaps risks missing the broader issue of predatory behavior across all ages.
