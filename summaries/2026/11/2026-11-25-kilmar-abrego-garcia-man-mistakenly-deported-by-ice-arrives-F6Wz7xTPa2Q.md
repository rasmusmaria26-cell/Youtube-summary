# Kilmar Abrego Garcia Leaves U.S. for Spain

- **Published:** 2026-11-25
- **Channel:** CBS News

## Summary
Kilmar Abrego Garcia, an El Salvador native and U.S. resident mistakenly deported under the Trump administration, has successfully arrived in Spain after a lengthy legal saga and administrative disputes.

## Major Developments
### 1. Arrival in Spain
Kilmar Abrego Garcia arrived in Madrid, Spain, alongside his wife and child, following a court order from the previous week.

**Why it matters:** It marks the conclusion of a high-profile deportation saga where he leaves for a country of his choosing under his own terms.

### 2. Case Timeline and Legal Disputes
Abrego Garcia was arrested in March 2019, faced a mistaken deportation to El Salvador in March 2025, was returned to the U.S. in June 2025 following a Supreme Court order, and eventually faced criminal charges that were dismissed by a judge as politically motivated.

**Why it matters:** His case became a major flashpoint in debates regarding immigration crackdowns and administrative mistakes under the Trump administration.

## Key Takeaways
- Kilmar Abrego Garcia successfully traveled to Spain with his American citizen wife and child.
- The Trump administration admitted to a mistake in deporting him to El Salvador initially.
- Criminal charges against him were dismissed by a judge who found them politically motivated.
