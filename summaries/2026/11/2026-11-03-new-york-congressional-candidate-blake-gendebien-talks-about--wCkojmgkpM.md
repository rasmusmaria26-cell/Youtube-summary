### 1. NY-21 Congressional Race and Voter Issues
Democratic candidate Blake Gendebien and Republican candidate Anthony Constantino are competing for New York's 21st district seat. Gendebien highlights voter concerns regarding high costs for gasoline, fuel, fertilizer, and heating oil.
