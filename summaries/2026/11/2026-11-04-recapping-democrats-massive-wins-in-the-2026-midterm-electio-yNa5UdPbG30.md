## Summary
CBS News projects that Democrats take back control of both the Senate and the House of Representatives in the 2026 midterm elections, defeating Republicans in key battleground races amidst high voter turnout and localized election challenges.

## Major Developments
### 1. Democrats Gain Control of Senate and House
CBS News projects Democrats take control of both the Senate and the House of Representatives during the 2026 midterms, defeating Republicans in key battleground races.

**Why it matters:** This shift represents a significant change in the balance of power in Washington, impacting the future legislative agenda and policy enforcement.

### 2. Historic Gubernatorial and Senate Victories
Key victories include Keisha Lance Bottoms projected as the first Black woman governor of Georgia, Roy Cooper winning a Senate seat flip in North Carolina, Byron Donalds becoming Florida's first Black governor, and Gina Hinojosa becoming Texas's first Latina governor.

**Why it matters:** These wins mark historic milestones for representation in state leadership and demonstrate significant political shifts in historically competitive states.

### 3. Voter Turnout and Logistical Challenges
Millions of Americans headed to the polls amid record early voter turnout. Isolated logistical issues occurred, including malfunctioning ballot machines in Westchester County (New York), paper ballot shortages in Chicago, and severe weather and flooding in South Florida.

**Why it matters:** Understanding turnout and operational hiccups helps assess the reliability of election processes and voter engagement levels.
