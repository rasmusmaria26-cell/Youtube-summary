## Summary
A 39-year-old postal employee was identified as the suspect in a shooting at the main post office in Des Moines, Iowa. Three victims were injured, including two female postal employees who self-transported to a nearby hospital and a male employee injured while fleeing. All victims are expected to recover. Police apprehended the suspect without incident 11 minutes after the first 911 call.

## Major Developments
### 1. Des Moines Post Office Shooting
A 39-year-old postal service employee opened fire with a 9mm gun on the second floor of the main post office in Des Moines, Iowa, injuring three people. Police arrived within three minutes of the first 911 call and apprehended the suspect along a nearby bike path within 11 minutes.
