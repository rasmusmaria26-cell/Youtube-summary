# Fallout from Thanksgiving Day Parade postponement

- **Published:** 2026-11-27
- **Channel:** CBS News

## Summary
The Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade was postponed until Saturday due to severe weather conditions, marking its first weather postponement in its 100-year history.

## Major Developments
### 1. Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade Postponed
New York City's Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade was postponed until Saturday due to high winds and weather conditions exceeding safety limits for giant balloons.

**Why it matters:** It is the parade's first weather postponement in its 100-year history, impacting tens of thousands of participants and spectators, including bands that traveled thousands of miles.

## Key Takeaways
- The parade was moved to Saturday due to wind gusts expected up to 65 mph, surpassing the 34 mph safety limit for giant balloons.
- This is the first time in 100 years that the parade has been postponed due to weather.
- Performers and bands, such as the Hawaii All-State Marching Band, faced adjustments after traveling thousands of miles for the event.
