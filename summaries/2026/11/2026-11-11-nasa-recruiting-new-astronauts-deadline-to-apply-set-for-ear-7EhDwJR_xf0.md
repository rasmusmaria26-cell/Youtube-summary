# NASA Recruiting New Class of Astronauts

- **Published:** 2026-11-11
- **Channel:** CBS News
- **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7EhDwJR_xf0)

## Summary
NASA is recruiting its next crop of astronauts with the application window closing on December 3, as the space agency focuses on lunar missions, building a moon base, and future travel to Mars.

## Major Developments
### 1. NASA Astronaut Recruitment and Requirements
NASA is currently accepting applications for new astronaut candidates. Requirements include U.S. citizenship, a master's degree in a STEM field (such as engineering, biological science, physical science, or computer science), and at least three years of related professional experience.

**Why it matters:** This recruitment drive supports future long-term missions to the Moon and Mars as space exploration expands beyond low Earth orbit.

## Key Takeaways
- Applications for the new NASA astronaut class close on December 3.
- Applicants must hold a master's degree in a STEM field and have at least three years of related experience.
- Future missions will focus heavily on establishing a permanent lunar base and preparing for Mars.
