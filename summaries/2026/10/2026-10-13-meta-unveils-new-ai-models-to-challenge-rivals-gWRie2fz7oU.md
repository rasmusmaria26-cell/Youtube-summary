# Meta Unveils New AI Models to Challenge Rivals

- **Published:** 2026-10-13
- **Channel:** CBS News
- **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gWRie2fz7oU)

## Summary
Meta has launched two new AI models, Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full, designed for tasks such as coding, memory, and AI agents. The company claims these models match or outperform rival offerings from OpenAI and Anthropic on benchmark tests.

## Major Developments
### 1. Meta Launches Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full
Meta unveiled two new AI models named Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full, targeted at tasks including coding, memory, and AI agents, alongside a consumer app named Muse available on the App Store.

**Why it matters:** Meta is positioning its new models to compete directly with leading industry systems from OpenAI and Anthropic, claiming benchmark performance on par with or exceeding rivals.

## Key Takeaways
- Meta launched Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full as its biggest milestone in the past year and a half.
- The models are integrated into a consumer app called Muse, available for free with paid tiers ranging from 20 to 100 dollars per month for advanced access.
- Meta's benchmarks place its models on par with offerings from OpenAI and Anthropic, showing strength in coding, health, financial modeling, and legal expertise.
