# Suspected Chinese Agent Arrested in Los Angeles

- **Published:** 2026-10-06
- **Channel:** CBS News
- **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wG45UuhF_S8)

## Summary
FBI arrests a California real estate agent, Wanying Heather Zhang, accused of acting as an unregistered agent of a foreign government and surveilling the son of Taiwan's president in the United States.

## Major Developments
### 1. Arrest of Suspected Chinese Agent
Federal law enforcement arrested Wanying Heather Zhang at Los Angeles International Airport on Sunday before she boarded a flight to Shanghai, China. She is charged with acting as an unregistered agent of a foreign government.

**Why it matters:** The case highlights ongoing national security concerns and federal investigations into alleged foreign espionage and intelligence operations targeting individuals associated with foreign leaders on U.S. soil.

### 2. Allegations of Surveillance Against Taiwanese President's Son
According to court documents and an FBI counterintelligence complaint, Zhang and an accomplice travelled to Seattle in September 2025 to take pictures at the suburban home of Ting Yu Lei, the son of Taiwanese President Ching-te Lai. Text messages and iCloud data retrieved by the FBI indicate Zhang reported back to her Chinese government handler.

**Why it matters:** It demonstrates how foreign intelligence operations allegedly utilize local operatives to conduct physical surveillance on high-profile foreign nationals living within the United States, amidst ongoing geopolitical tensions involving Taiwan and China.

## Key Takeaways
- Wanying Heather Zhang was arrested at LAX while preparing to fly to Shanghai.
- She is charged with acting as an unregistered agent of a foreign government.
- The FBI complaint alleges she surveilled Ting Yu Lei, the son of Taiwan's president, in September 2025.
- Evidence cited in court filings includes text messages on WeChat and location data obtained via search warrants.
