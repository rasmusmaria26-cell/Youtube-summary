## Summary
Students at Cornell University held a rally to protest the school's handling of a 2024 alleged gang rape case involving a student referred to as Jane Doe, as well as the initial investigation and legal proceedings surrounding the incident.

## Major Developments
### 1. Cornell University Student Protest
Students gathered at Cornell University to condemn the school's handling of an alleged 2024 gang rape case and to support Jane Doe.

**Why it matters:** It highlights student outrage over institutional accountability and transparency regarding sexual assault investigations on college campuses.

### 2. Reopened Criminal Investigation
An open criminal investigation was initiated by New York Attorney General Letitia James following civil litigation filed by Jane Doe against fraternity members and the school.

**Why it matters:** New scrutiny on the actions of police and district attorney offices has emerged after questions arose regarding interviews with suspects and the handling of evidence.
