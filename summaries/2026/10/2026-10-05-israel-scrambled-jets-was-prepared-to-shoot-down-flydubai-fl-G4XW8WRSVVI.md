# Israel Scrambled Jets and Prepared to Shoot Down FlyDubai Flight

- **Published:** 2026-10-05
- **Channel:** CBS News
- **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4XW8WRSVVI)

## Summary
According to CBS News, a FlyDubai flight from Dubai to Tel Aviv nearly crashed after an attempted hijacking by a co-pilot. Israeli officials were prepared to shoot down the plane carrying 172 people, mostly Israelis, to prevent a 9/11-style attack on Ben Gurion International Airport.

## Major Developments
### 1. FlyDubai Flight Hijacking Attempt
A FlyDubai co-pilot attempted to hijack flight 1073 and intended to crash the plane into Tel Aviv's Ben Gurion International Airport. Passengers stormed the cockpit and stopped the incident less than 30 seconds before a potential crash.

**Why it matters:** The near-disaster posed an imminent threat to hundreds of lives and potential large-scale destruction comparable to the 9/11 attacks.

### 2. Israeli Response and Airspace Security
Israeli officials shut down their airspace and scrambled fighter jets, prepared to shoot down the plane before it entered Israeli airspace to prevent a terrorist attack.

**Why it matters:** Demonstrates the extreme security measures and military readiness of Israel to intercept and destroy compromised commercial aircraft to protect national infrastructure.

## Key Takeaways
- A FlyDubai co-pilot allegedly attempted to execute a 9/11-style attack by crashing a flight into Ben Gurion Airport.
- Passengers successfully intervened in the cockpit just seconds before a catastrophic crash.
- Israeli military forces scrambled jets and were prepared to shoot down the passenger plane to avert disaster.
