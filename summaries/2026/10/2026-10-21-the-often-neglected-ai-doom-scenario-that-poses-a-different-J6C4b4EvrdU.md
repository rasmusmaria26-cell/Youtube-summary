# The AI Doom Scenario: Gradual Disempowerment

- **Published:** 2026-10-21
- **Channel:** CBS News
- **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J6C4b4EvrdU)

## Summary
CBS News interviews Robocurve CEO Jay Chooi about the concept of gradual disempowerment, where AI systems become faster, cheaper, and better than humans without requiring malicious intent or evil agents.

## Major Developments
### 1. Gradual Disempowerment by AI
As AI advances to become cheaper, faster, and more capable than humans, society may lose its incentive to keep humans around, similar to how modern horses became less necessary once cars were invented.

**Why it matters:** It presents a plausible risk where humanity is gradually replaced without any malicious actors, rogue agents, or Terminator-style villains.

### 2. Shifting Hiring Trends at Anthropic
Anthropic employees previously expressed concerns regarding existential threats, and surveys show employees expect AI to handle 75 percent of their jobs by the end of the decade. Anthropic's junior staff hiring dropped from 27% to 9% in under two years.

**Why it matters:** It demonstrates early workplace shifts indicating that companies are already reducing human hiring as AI capability increases.

## Key Takeaways
- Gradual disempowerment does not require evil AI or human villains, just superior AI capability.
- Comparisons are drawn to historical evolutionary shifts like Homo sapiens and Neanderthals.
- Anthropic internal hiring data shows a notable drop in junior staff onboarding as AI adoption grows.
