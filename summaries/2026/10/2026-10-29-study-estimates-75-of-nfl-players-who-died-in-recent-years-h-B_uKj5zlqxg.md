# Study Estimates 75% of Former NFL Players Who Died Had CTE

- **Published:** 2026-10-29
- **Channel:** CBS News
- **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B_uKj5zlqxg)

## Summary
A new study published by JAMA Network Open reveals that 75 percent of former NFL players who died between 2008 and 2023 suffered from chronic traumatic encephalopathy (CTE), a degenerative brain disease caused by repetitive head impacts.

## Major Developments
### 1. New Study on CTE in Former NFL Players
Research published by JAMA Network Open found that 75% of ex-NFL players who died between 2008 and 2023 had CTE. The condition can only be diagnosed after death and is linked to repeated head impacts.

**Why it matters:** It provides significant evidence of the causal link between repetitive head impacts, duration of play, and the risk of CTE, highlighting the severity of the issue in contact sports.

## Key Takeaways
- 75% of former NFL players who died between 2008 and 2023 suffered from CTE.
- CTE can currently only be diagnosed post-mortem.
- Researchers advocate for preventative measures, such as reducing tackle football before high school and minimizing head impacts at all levels.
