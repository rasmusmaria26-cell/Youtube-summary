# AI Whistleblowers Warn Technology is Outpacing Safety Measures

- **Published:** 2026-10-06
- **Channel:** CBS News
- **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X9kmzhqsoRw)

## Summary
Former employees from leading AI companies including Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google DeepMind warned New York City lawmakers that rapidly developing AI technology is outpacing safety measures and called for increased government oversight.

## Major Developments
### 1. AI Whistleblowers Testify at NYC Council Hearing
During an oversight hearing before the New York City Council, former employees of Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google DeepMind described what they termed a negligent culture within the AI industry regarding safety protocols.

**Why it matters:** It highlights growing internal concerns and public testimony from industry insiders about the lack of sufficient safety controls in major AI labs.

### 2. Calls for Federal Oversight on Advanced AI Systems
Connor Leahy, U.S. Executive Director of Control AI and former engineer/whistleblower, argued that advanced AI systems pose national security threats and require government oversight rather than corporate self-regulation.

**Why it matters:** It underscores the argument that private companies should not solely govern high-risk technologies that could impact public safety and national security.

## Key Takeaways
- Former employees from major AI firms warn that AI development is moving faster than safety measures.
- Whistleblowers describe a negligent culture regarding safety within top AI companies.
- Experts argue that AI systems are becoming adversarial tools that require strict government oversight, comparable to nuclear or chemical regulation.
