# Takeaways from interview with Justice Samuel Alito as new Supreme Court term begins

- **Published:** 2026-10-05
- **Channel:** CBS News
- **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=niWlioJGKKM)

## Summary
CBS News Chief Legal Correspondent Jan Crawford discusses her extensive interview with Supreme Court Justice Samuel Alito ahead of the new Supreme Court term, covering major legal milestones, the fallout from leaked draft opinions like Dobbs, and security concerns.

## Major Developments
### 1. Supreme Court Term Opens with Contentious Cases
The Supreme Court is beginning a new term featuring cases on climate change, assault weapons bans, immigration, religious rights, and voting laws.

**Why it matters:** These divisive cases are expected to deeply test the court and potentially divide justices further.

### 2. Justice Alito on the Dobbs Leak and Security Fallout
Justice Alito discussed the unprecedented leak of the Dobbs draft opinion, noting it created a sense of shock and put justices at risk of assassination and targeted threats.

**Why it matters:** The leak cast a pall over the court, damaged internal relationships, and introduced significant personal danger to the justices.

## Key Takeaways
- Justice Alito has served for 20 years and his conservative legal positions on abortion, affirmative action, and religious liberty have become the law of the land.
- Alito considered retirement earlier in the year but decided against it.
- The upcoming Supreme Court term will involve major ideological clashes on key American issues.
