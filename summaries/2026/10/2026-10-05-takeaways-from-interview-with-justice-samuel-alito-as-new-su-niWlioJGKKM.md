The Supreme Court is starting a new term considering cases regarding climate change, assault weapons bans, immigration, religious rights, and voting laws.

**Why it matters:** These cases involve major national policy questions that will significantly impact society and legal precedents.

**Why it matters:** Alito is one of the most consequential justices, and his positions on abortion, affirmative action, and religious liberty have become foundational to current law.
