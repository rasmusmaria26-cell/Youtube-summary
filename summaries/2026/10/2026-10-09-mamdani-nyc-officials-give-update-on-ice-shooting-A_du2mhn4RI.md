## Summary
New York City officials, including the mayor, police commissioner, and attorney general, addressed an incident where ICE agents shot a man in a car in Marble Hill with a five-year-old child inside, leading to calls to abolish ICE.

## Major Developments
### 1. ICE Shooting in Marble Hill
Around 4 PM, federal immigration agents surrounded a vehicle in Marble Hill wearing construction vests and face masks, then opened fire as a five-year-old child sat in the back seat. The child was physically unharmed, but the 28-year-old driver, Oscar Belgall, was shot in the neck and taken to New York Presbyterian Allen Hospital in stable condition. At least one ICE agent discharged seven rounds. NYPD officers arrived subsequently to secure the scene and preserve evidence.

**Why it matters:** This incident highlights severe tensions between local and federal authorities regarding immigration enforcement tactics, public safety concerns, and the impact of federal operations on residential neighborhoods.

## Key Takeaways
- A 28-year-old man was shot by ICE agents in Marble Hill during a targeted federal operation.
- A five-year-old child in the back seat was physically unharmed and later found safe with family.
- Local officials condemned the actions, stating that ICE operations undermine public safety and demanding that ICE leave New York City.
