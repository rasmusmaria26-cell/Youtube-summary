# US and German Scientists Win Nobel Medicine Prize for Optogenetics Discoveries

- **Published:** 2026-10-06
- **Channel:** CBS News
- **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dhxsGW7Tbiw)

## Summary
U.S. scientist Karl Deisseroth and two German scientists won the Nobel Prize in Medicine for their groundbreaking discoveries in optogenetics, a technique that uses light to control brain cell activity.

## Major Developments
### 1. Nobel Prize in Medicine Awarded for Optogenetics
American neuroscientist and psychiatrist Karl Deisseroth, alongside two German scientists (Peter Hegemann and Georg Nagel), won the Nobel Prize in Medicine for their work in optogenetics.

**Why it matters:** Optogenetics allows scientists to use light to control specific brain cells with precision up to thousands of a second, shifting brain research from correlation to causation and offering potential pathways to better understand and treat psychiatric and neurological illnesses.

## Key Takeaways
- Optogenetics enables precise control of brain cells using light-sensitive proteins derived from organisms like algae.
- The technique permits researchers to test cause-and-effect relationships in brain circuits related to memory, emotion, and movement.
- Early human studies have shown partial recovery of vision in patients with severe retinal disease using direct treatment.
