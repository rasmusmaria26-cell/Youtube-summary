# Inside Scotland's Anti-Migrant Backlash

- **Published:** 2026-10-12
- **Channel:** CBS News
- **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Kuvc1N5DvT0)

## Summary
This video report explores the growing anti-immigration protests and community tensions in Glasgow, Scotland, detailing housing crises, increased arrivals of asylum seekers, rising crime statistics, and the emergence of vigilante groups.

## Major Developments
### 1. Housing Emergencies and Asylum Seeker Arrivals
Glasgow City Council declared a housing emergency in 2023 due to pressures on homelessness services exacerbated by thousands of new asylum seekers and refugees arriving over three years. Over 47% of Glasgow's 7,244 homeless households are refugees, while local organizations like Homeless Project Scotland face severe operational strain.

**Why it matters:** Demonstrates the direct logistical and resource pressures placed on local UK municipalities by high volumes of asylum seekers.

### 2. Rising Crime and Misinformation
Police Scotland statistics show recorded crime in Glasgow rose by 5%, sexual crime by 7%, and rape/attempted rape cases by 43% since 2022. Scottish police do not record nationality, meaning there is no official link between the crime rise and migrants, but social media posts and news reports of migrant crimes have fueled discontent and misinformation.

**Why it matters:** Illustrates how rising local crime rates combined with social media misinformation can amplify community tensions and xenophobic sentiment.

### 3. Protests and Vigilante Activity
Growing public discontent has led to protests, counter-protests, and the formation of vigilante groups like 'Scots Active' who patrol streets claiming police are not doing enough. Arson attacks on vacant buildings designated for migrant housing have been described by local officials as fueled by online lies and malicious media.

**Why it matters:** Shows the escalation of public frustration into localized vigilantism and extreme ideological actions, posing safety risks to communities.

## Key Takeaways
- Glasgow's local resources and housing services are under extreme strain following the arrival of thousands of refugees and asylum seekers.
- While local crime rates have increased, Scottish police statistics do not track nationality, meaning links to migration remain speculative.
- Community discontent and unverified social media information have provided openings for extremist and vigilante groups to gain traction.
