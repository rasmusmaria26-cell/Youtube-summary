# Alito Slams Criticism of Supreme Court from Political Leaders

- **Published:** 2026-10-06
- **Channel:** CBS News
- **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UoPwMEmn0vg)

## Summary
Supreme Court Justice Samuel Alito discusses the damaging impact of criticism from political leaders on the judiciary's legitimacy, while legal analysts review upcoming Supreme Court cases including a major climate change lawsuit.

## Major Developments
### 1. Justice Alito on Political Criticism of the Supreme Court
Justice Samuel Alito stated in an interview with CBS News Chief Legal Correspondent Jan Crawford that political leaders criticizing Supreme Court rulings can be quite damaging to the court and its legitimacy.

**Why it matters:** It highlights tensions between the judiciary and political figures, addressing concerns over public perception and institutional trust.

### 2. Supreme Court Term Begins with Climate Change Case
The Supreme Court began its new term hearing oral arguments on a climate change lawsuit involving Suncor, Exxon, and the City and County of Boulder regarding whether cities and counties can hold energy companies responsible for climate damages.

**Why it matters:** This case could determine whether such climate-related lawsuits belong in state courts under state law or federal courts, potentially reshaping accountability for greenhouse gas emissions.

## Key Takeaways
- Justice Alito views political criticism of the Supreme Court as damaging to the court's public standing.
- Legal analysts note a shift in public critique moving from disagreement with specific decisions to attacks on the court's integrity.
- The new Supreme Court term features cases on climate change, immigration, Second Amendment rights, and religious freedom.
