## Summary
Former Cornell University fraternity members who graduated between 2022 and 2023 are asking the university to publicly confirm they were not involved in a 2024 incident or part of the group chat in question, citing professional fallout and online threats.

## Major Developments
### 1. Former Fraternity Members Deny Involvement
Former members of the Chi Phi fraternity at Cornell who graduated between 2022 and 2023 sent a letter asking the university to publicly confirm they were not part of the group chat or the October 2024 incident involving allegations by Jane Doe.

**Why it matters:** These former students are facing professional consequences, adverse actions from employers or higher education institutions, and online threats due to being falsely associated with the scandal.
