## Summary
The State Department advised Americans to use caution in France due to recent violence and looting during widespread European protests over the cost of living. Despite hundreds of thousands of protesters marching and clashes with police in Paris, thousands of American travelers attended the first regular-season NFL game in France between the Pittsburgh Steelers and the New Orleans Saints.

## Major Developments
### 1. European Protests Over Cost of Living
Thousands of people across Europe, including Italy, Albania, and France, participated in protests over the high cost of living. In Paris, over 400,000 people marched, which largely remained peaceful before turning violent with clashes between some protesters and 70,000 deployed police officers.

**Why it matters:** The widespread protests highlight growing civil unrest across Europe driven by economic pressures and the rising cost of living.

### 2. First Regular-Season NFL Game in France
The Pittsburgh Steelers played against the New Orleans Saints in Paris for the first regular-season NFL game held in France, drawing thousands of American fans despite security advisories and local protests.

**Why it matters:** This landmark international sporting event brought major American sports culture to France amidst a backdrop of severe local civil unrest.
